Main-menu presentation
The valley keep illustration in assets/mature/menu/valley_keep.png is original
AI-generated menu artwork created for this project. It is an illustration,
not a gameplay screenshot. The heraldic mark and menu shader are original.
No Age of Empires or Company of Heroes artwork is included. No new public
license is granted for original game artwork.

Regular, licensed under SIL OFL 1.1. Its full upstream copyright and license

is Valley Crown Display. Existing Noto and DejaVu notices remain unchanged.
