Valley & Crown / 霧谷王旗
Experimental Web candidate vc-43276605e0fb

This candidate contains the existing integrated game source and native character assets. It is not a production release. Native tests and reproducible exports do not establish browser, Safari, physical-phone, frame-rate, or artistic acceptance. Known limitations include pale/patchy character materials, small landscape actors, and subtle early construction foundations. No lighting or camera prototype is included.
